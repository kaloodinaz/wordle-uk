
# Wordle UK

![Wordle UK](https://kaloodinaz.github.io/wordle-uk/wordle.jpg)

**[Wordle UK](https://wordlegame.uk/)** is a fun and challenging online word puzzle game where players test their vocabulary and word-solving skills by guessing a hidden five-letter word. Inspired by the popular Wordle format, the game offers a simple yet addictive daily word challenge that anyone can enjoy directly in their browser.

Try to discover the secret word in just six attempts. After each guess, the tiles change color to provide helpful clues. Green means the letter is in the correct position, yellow indicates that the letter is in the word but in the wrong position, and grey shows that the letter is not included in the hidden word.

Wordle UK is easy to play, requires no downloads or registration, and works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. Whether you enjoy daily brain teasers or want to improve your English vocabulary, it's a great way to challenge yourself and have fun.

### How to Play

- Guess the hidden five-letter word.
- Enter a valid word and submit your guess.
- You have six attempts to find the correct answer.
- Use the color hints to narrow down possible words.
- Find the secret word before running out of attempts!
